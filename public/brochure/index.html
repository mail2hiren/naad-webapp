Why Naad

Built for the people who run the hospital, and the people who heal in it.

One record, every department

Reception, doctors, pharmacy, physiotherapy and the ward work from the same live record. No re-typing, no paper slips lost between floors.

Every rupee traceable

Visits, pharmacy sales and bed-days are billed where they happen. Reports show what was billed, collected and still owed, by doctor and by day.

Ready for an audit

Every action is written to a tamper-evident log you can check with one tap. Each hospital sees only its own data, kept in India.

Eyes on the patient

Listen and examine while Naad keeps the record. No screen between you and the person in front of you.

Finish clinic on time

Notes are drafted as the visit ends, not typed up after the last patient has gone home.

Never miss a question

Case-specific prompts remind you what to ask or examine, such as "Does the pain travel down the leg?"

You stay in charge

The AI drafts. The clinician decides.

You sign every note

Naad drafts, a clinician approves. No sign-off, no record.

It never diagnoses

No diagnosis and no examination findings are written by the AI. Impressions are marked tentative.

Consent first

Recording consent is asked and saved for every visit. Typing always works as a fallback.

Each hospital, its own data

The database itself keeps hospitals apart. Patients see only their own records in their app.

Tamper-evident trail

Key actions are chained so any change shows. The admin can verify the log in one tap.

Spend you control

A monthly AI budget per hospital. Past it, staff simply type; nothing stops.
